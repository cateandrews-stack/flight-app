Model Transparency

  • Assumption: The score only considers price, total travel time, and number of stops, and it assumes your slider setting reflects what matters most to you.
  • Limitation: Flight data comes from a single API search, so prices and availability can change. Scores are relative to the flights returned, not every flight that exists.
  • What it does NOT mean: A high score does not mean this is the best flight for everyone. The app does not account for baggage fees, airline quality, layover airports, or departure times.
  • What it does mean: The recommendation is a decision aid that ranks the options shown based on your preferences. It does not book flights or guarantee the listed price.

Formula: Overall = Price weight x Price score + Time weight x Time score + Stops weight x Stops score. Price weight = slider / 10; Time weight = 60% of the remaining weight; Stops weight = 40% of the remaining weight. Stops score: nonstop 100, 1 stop 60, 2+ stops 20.
